# Privacy Policy for Wayo

**Last updated:** August 4, 2026

Wayo ("the app", "we", "us") is developed by MSLabs. This Privacy Policy explains what information Wayo accesses, how it is used, and your choices — in plain language.

## Summary

Wayo helps you remember where you parked. It works entirely on your device. We do not operate any servers, we do not collect your data, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone.

## Information We Access

### Location (GPS)
Wayo uses your device's precise (GPS) and approximate location to:
- Record the coordinates of your parking spot when you tap "Park Here."
- Show your live position and direction while guiding you back to that spot.

Location data is stored **only on your device** (in a local database) and is never transmitted anywhere. You can delete a saved spot at any time from within the app, which permanently removes its location data from your device.

### Camera
Wayo uses your device's camera to take a photo of your parking spot. Camera access is required to use the app — Wayo will not proceed without it. The photo is saved **only on your device's local storage** and is attached to that parking record so you can recognize the spot later. Photos are never uploaded or shared by the app.

### Vibration
Wayo uses your device's vibration motor to give haptic feedback (e.g., when you arrive at your parked car). This does not involve any data collection.

### Compass / Orientation Sensors
Wayo uses your device's compass (magnetometer) and motion sensors, where available, to point you toward your parking spot. This data is processed on-device in real time and is not stored or transmitted.

## Data Storage

All parking spot data (location, notes, and photos) is stored locally on your device using a local database. This data:
- Is never sent to us or to any third party.
- Stays on your device until you delete it (either by using the app's delete function or by uninstalling the app).
- May be included in your device's own backup system (e.g., Android's built-in backup to your personal Google account), which is controlled by your device settings, not by us.

## In-App Purchases

Wayo offers an optional "Pro" upgrade via Google Play's in-app billing system. Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment information — Google's own privacy policy governs that data: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Third-Party Services

Wayo does not integrate any advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. The only third-party service involved is Google Play Billing, used solely to process optional Pro upgrade purchases as described above.

## Data Sharing

We do not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share any of your data, because Wayo does not have servers and does not send app data off your device.

## Children's Privacy

Wayo does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, since all data stays on-device and nothing is transmitted to us.

## Your Choices

- Location and camera permissions are both required for Wayo to function — the app cannot mark or find a parking spot without them, so it will prompt you for both and will not proceed until they're granted.
- You can delete individual parking records (including photos) from within the app at any time.
- You can clear all app data or uninstall the app to remove everything Wayo has stored, directly from your device settings.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this same page with an updated "Last updated" date.
